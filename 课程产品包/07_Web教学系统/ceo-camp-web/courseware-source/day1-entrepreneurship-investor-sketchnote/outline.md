<STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS>
Design Aesthetic: Soft hand-drawn sketchnote illustration with warm classroom storybook energy. The deck should feel like a smart, playful notebook page mixed with a picture book spread: clear scenes, hand-drawn arrows, sticky notes, star coins, circles, and small doodles. It must be child-friendly, concrete, and full of scene details, never corporate, technical, or dark.

Background:
  Texture: Warm off-white paper grain, subtle and friendly.
  Base Color: Warm Off-White (#FAF8F0).

Typography:
  Headlines: Large bold hand-written marker lettering, uneven enough to feel hand-drawn, still highly readable.
  Body: Clear rounded handwritten labels, short and legible. Use only a few Chinese words per label.

Color Palette:
  Primary Text: Deep Charcoal (#2C3E50).
  Accent 1: Soft Orange (#F4A261).
  Accent 2: Mustard Yellow (#E9C46A).
  Accent 3: Sage Green (#87A96B).
  Accent 4: Light Blue (#7EC8E3).
  Accent 5: Coral Red (#E76F51).

Visual Elements:
  - Hand-drawn classroom cards, star coins, sticky notes, magnifying glasses, sketch circles.
  - Warm child characters with clear actions and emotions.
  - Booth cards or project cards, but not adult expo booths.
  - Wavy arrows and connector lines, never perfect geometry.
  - Soft color fills that do not need to fully cover outlines.

Density Guidelines:
  - One clear message per slide.
  - Max 3-4 visible text elements when possible.
  - Large visual first, text as labels.
  - Generous whitespace so a child can understand the page from the back of a classroom.

Style Rules:
  Do: Keep visuals concrete, warm, vivid, and easy to read. Let scenes explain the product need.
  Don't: Do not use photorealism, dark sci-fi, adult business style, complex software UI, page numbers, footers, logos, dense paragraphs, or teacher-script text.
</STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS>
