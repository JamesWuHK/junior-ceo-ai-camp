<STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS>
Design Aesthetic: Soft hand-drawn sketchnote illustration with warm classroom energy. The deck should feel like a smart, playful notebook page: clear pictures, hand-drawn arrows, sticky notes, circles, and small doodles. It must be child-friendly and concrete, not corporate, technical, or dark.

Background:
  Texture: Warm off-white paper grain, subtle and friendly.
  Base Color: Warm Off-White (#FAF8F0).

Typography:
  Headlines: Large bold hand-written marker lettering, uneven enough to feel hand-drawn, still highly readable.
  Body: Clear rounded handwritten labels, short and legible. Use only a few Chinese words per label.

Color Palette:
  Primary Text: Deep Charcoal (#2C3E50) - titles and main labels.
  Background: Warm Off-White (#FAF8F0) - page background.
  Accent 1: Soft Orange (#F4A261) - key highlights and warm emphasis.
  Accent 2: Mustard Yellow (#E9C46A) - sticky notes and labels.
  Accent 3: Sage Green (#87A96B) - learning and growth notes.
  Accent 4: Light Blue (#7EC8E3) - AI brain, tech clues, soft glow.
  Accent 5: Red Brown (#A0522D) - pencil lines, desk details, warm contrast.

Visual Elements:
  - Hand-drawn robot head with a visible friendly brain inside.
  - Sticky notes, task cards, clue cards, photo frames, magnifying glasses.
  - Wavy arrows and connector lines, never perfect geometry.
  - Soft color fills that do not need to fully cover outlines.
  - Doodle stars, circles, underlines, and small observation marks.

Density Guidelines:
  - One clear message per slide.
  - Max 3-4 visible text elements when possible.
  - Large visual first, text as labels.
  - Generous whitespace so a child can understand the page from the back of a classroom.

Style Rules:
  Do: Keep visuals concrete, warm, and easy to read. Use hand-drawn diagrams and story scenes. Let pictures explain the concept.
  Don't: Do not use photorealism, dark sci-fi, adult business style, complex software UI, page numbers, footers, logos, dense paragraphs, or teacher-script text.
</STYLE_INSTRUCTIONS>
